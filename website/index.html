Catalogue 01

One dependable workshop

Built for the whole archive.

VPKMERGE handles the practical work around Source 2 assets, from load-order consolidation to finished, drop-in addon packages.

01

Merge & split

Consolidate many mods into one VPK, preview collisions, and route one archive back into precise feature-level packages.

  • Deterministic load order
  • Strict collision policies
  • Path-based split plans
02

Inspect & decode

Walk the file tree and turn compiled Source 2 resources into formats you can actually examine, compare, and use.

  • Texture previews
  • Model export to glTF
  • Readable soundevents
03

Transform & rebuild

Recolor VFX, replace artwork and audio, edit materials, and pack the results at the exact paths the game expects.

  • Texture and VFX recolor
  • Custom icons and audio
  • Standalone addon output
